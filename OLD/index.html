Gareth's Laws of Property

A deal that is 99% done is not done.

That 1% is everything.

The Casco Viejo buyer and the Costa del Este buyer are not the same person.

Strategy changes with the street.

Your broker should know the zoning, the yield and the exit.

Not just the beach nearby.

If you have to lean off the balcony to see the ocean, it's not an ocean view.

A good view should not require imagination.

Client matters are not public matters.

Discretion is part of the service.

If the lease is not understood, the asset is not understood.

The real value is in the fine print.

The most expensive mistake is rushing.

The second most expensive is hesitating.

Most deals don't fail on price.

They fail on expectations.

"Not in a rush" usually means "not at that price."

Timing hides the real objection.

Never make the listing prettier than the building.

Tell the truth in words and photos.

We don't list our properties on purpose.

Portals are useful. We use them too. But many of our best opportunities are off-market or privately shared. Tell us what you're looking for and we'll show you more.
